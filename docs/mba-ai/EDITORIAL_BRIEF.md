# Wharton MBA AI landing page: Editorial and production brief

## Strategic intent
Primary audience: prospective full-time two-year MBA applicants and admitted-student yield. Secondary: current and future-pathway students. The page complements, but does not reproduce, the five-message unfinished-application campaign.

## Thesis
**The Advantage Is What You Do With AI.** At a time when access to powerful tools is widespread, Wharton's value is rigorous business expertise, judgment, practical AI capability, and accountability.

## Voice
Institutional, confident, precise, premium. Shown through examples, not claims about being better than competitors. Inspired by MBA Vice Dean Michael Roberts' October 2026 filmed interview, **not** a first-person signed letter.

1. Pre-Term AI foundation and school-supported tools: [Wharton AI Pre-Term story](https://www.wharton.upenn.edu/whartons-mba-experience-begins-with-teamwork-leadership-and-ai/).

## Proposed original film beats
0–12 seconds: Everyone has access to AI. What sets leaders apart?
12–28 seconds: Business expertise, sound judgment, and accountability.
28–45 seconds: Students learn to build solutions rather than automate yesterday's workflow.
45–65 seconds: Research, industry, and the classroom create a continually renewing teaching loop.
65–85 seconds: Wharton scale with entrepreneurial agility.
Closing: The advantage is what you do with AI.
